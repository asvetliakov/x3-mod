  row `applied` with the vanilla bytes, a defer / park / release on one gate jump with the old track audible through the
  warp, a collapse on a second jump, the vanilla fallback on a combat start and on a save, a same-id loop with no queue
  row, and no `refuse reason=cells` on a `0x0db54` stop with the alert at 0/0.
- The frame-cell values are read for the first time in that flight (Run 189 logged depths only); a `cells` refusal row
  carries all six for a correction of the indices.
- Whether the loader's peephole pass (`0x0049e4f0`) leaves the `op6f; jt` window as the file has it is bound by the exact
  in-memory byte compare: a rewritten window refuses with the bytes seen and gate jumps keep the vanilla fade.
- Docking (`2001..2004`) and the MD's `DoPlayMusic` are deferrable like a sector change (section 8.3's note); add them to
  `immediate_id` if a dock must switch at once.
- Audible continuity of the orphan across its re-key and the gap at the release (about one frame plus the DirectSound
  prebuffer the vanilla same-id replay also has) are settled only by the flight.
