Length and brightness follow throttle exactly as in the game: the game stretches the jet to 0.25 + 1.75 × throttle, the proxy reads that scale from the draw, and at the default nozzle width (0.5 of the jet's value) the plume is 4 nozzle widths long at full throttle, as here. The sliders open at the game's look and keep their numbers and directions. Revised law (the game's since 2026-10-03, docs/architecture/engine-exhaust-look-critique.md): a thin hot core that cools along the plume inside a peaked body wrapped in a darker outer sheath that carries the visible width, the colour core → tint → darker tint, streaks 4.5 : 1 along the axis moving with the flow, shock cells that carve dark gaps (crests at the body's level), tongues at the edge and the tail, a tight halo at the mouth; Core radius now sets the hot core's width, Edge erosion the streaks' erosion and the tail's tongues, Diamond strength the gaps' depth (full at 0.59), Halo width its e-fold (0.32 × the value). Previous law is the slab look flown until then; A/B shows both (side view: previous on top; end-on: previous left). End-on is the game's nozzle disc for a plume pointing at the camera (the chase view): the body integrated along the axis (8 samples), the hot centre, the nozzle ring (3× its side radiance at the detail level 1, less on short plumes), radial streaks scrolling outward and the halo, under the game's soft cap. Detail level is the game's per-plume level, smoothstep(8, 40) of the nozzle's drawn width in px: at 0 the one law is a smooth soft profile of the previous law's width and energy (TAA keeps a small moving plume and the far dots their brightness), towards 1 the core thins and the structure and the outer flame come in. Game-only, not shown, the nozzle spill through the hull, the far-engine distance law and the end-on disc's distance dimming (0.5 at 20 px to 1 from 160 px), the RCS puff and brake flare attack, the SETA travel look, the mouth's hand-over between the disc and the side quad, and the game's tonemap: the lab maps x / (1 + x), the game AgX, which whitens bright bodies sooner, so judge the hue and the structure here and the brightness in flight.
