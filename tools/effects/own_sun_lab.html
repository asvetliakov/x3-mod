Own Sun Lab

A live mock-up of the mod's own sun pixel law (own_sun_ps.hlsl) in linear HDR through AgX and a light bloom. Tune it here; the numbers port one-to-one into own_sun_core.h. Drag the station across the sun.

The sun is the game's pixel law, constant for constant (src/effects/own_sun_ps.hlsl, own_sun_core.h pixel_constants()), drawn ONE/ONE into an engine-space scene, then decoded (gamma 2.2), exposed by 2EV and tone mapped with the minimal AgX of agx_reference.py. The station stands in for the depth lane: the nine knight taps clip each pixel, the thirteen core taps scale the glow. Pixel sizes follow the chosen game resolution, so the 3-px ray width floor and the 1.5-px draw floor behave as in flight. Approximate: the bloom (a few Kawase taps instead of the five-level pyramid), no TAA, no auto exposure, a still frame.
