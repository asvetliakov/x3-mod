What this draws, and what is approximated

  • Each slot plays one Effects row on the shared looping timeline (t = 0 is the spawn): muzzle (TBullets col 21), projectile (the col 0 body with the col 19 flight effect attached), impact (col 20) and the death explosion of the chosen tier (TShips col 47 by class, TDocks/TFactories col 14, TAsteroids col 8; 0 = default row). The left canvas is the vanilla reconstruction; the dashed right canvas is reserved for our replacement (renderOurs(record, t), currently empty).
  • An element is its body (numeric id N = objects/v/N, five digits) drawn from its LOD-0 triangles and UVs at uniform scale = element size: a body flat in x/y faces the camera; any other body (shockwave discs lie in x/z) is drawn as a 3D object seen from 30 degrees above its x/z plane (approximated: the engine orients it by the effect's carrier); it lives duration ms from timeoffset. Size: GROW min to max linearly; GROWANDSHRINK min, max at half-life, back to min (sine, approximated); otherwise max. rndsize and RANDOMSIZE shrink by up to that fraction (seeded). FADEOUT / FADEOUTBODY fade linearly to zero; RANDOMROT spins the sprite to a seeded angle. An element without TIMEOUT stays for the whole loop. Offsets, CUT, atten, halfspeed, NOZTEST, NOSKIP, WRECK, DEBRIS, PUSH and the row flags (NOROTATE, NODRIFT, HIDEOBJECT) are listed but not drawn (unknown or not visual here).
  • Blend per material: ONE/ONE as additive (canvas lighter), ONE/INVSRCCOLOR as screen (exactly src + dst(1 - src)), SRCALPHA/INVSRCALPHA as source-over with the alpha texture (when it is a real file; a texture that falls back to the NONE_* placeholder is ignored as an alpha source). Additive colour is the diffuse premultiplied by its own alpha (inferred; most diffuse maps have constant alpha). Classic and MATERIAL3 materials (no recorded blend) are drawn additive (approximated).
  • Texture animations (-N names, types/Animations) play their frames, each a sprite-sheet tile by its UV offset, evenly over the element's life (TAT_TAGONESHOT: once; frame durations are not read, approximated); on the projectile they cycle at 25 frames/s.
  • Projectile: the body as authored (its own crossed-card length and width, i.e. the vanilla elongation of the mesh), seen from the side with the flight axis to the right, rolled 30 degrees about it so both cards show. The node scale from cols 16-18 and the 0.1 x speed quad length are printed but not applied (their units against body units are not traced). The optional tint multiplies by cols 13-15 normalised to the brightest channel; it is off by default because these columns create the bullet's light (weapon-effect-spawn.md, cols 13-15) and the bolt atlases carry their own colour per tile (stock SS_BULLET_IRE: blue light, orange tile).
  • Every canvas fits its own content (scale printed in the corner), so sizes compare within a slot, not between slots. Zoom "fit whole life" keeps one scale for the loop (growth is visible, early frames can be tiny); "fit current time" refits every frame to the elements alive at t. LIGHT elements are scene lights in the engine: their body is not drawn (stock rows give them bodies such as objects/v/00002, a large mesh); when enabled they show as a faint radial glow of the element size. LENSFLARE elements get a generic streak flare (the flare tables are not read). PROPAGATE children spawn n in [min, max] copies of the child row at seeded delays in [min, max] delay after prop_delay, scattered over half the parent's extent (approximated). Particle generators and trails (Particles3) are not drawn.
  • Scenes. An absent numeric body objects/v/N falls back to objects/cut/N (inferred: the engine's search order for effect element bodies is not traced; its cut-scene loader names cut ids cut\%05d). Every such member is a scene (text P n; B id; parts with keys, or binary CUT1), as are the asteroid and debris bodies some rows name. A scene is drawn as one body: the first key of every part, each part body at its first-key position (scene units / 65536, inferred; CUT1 key positions are not decoded, those parts sit at the origin), without the key rotation and without the later keys, so the parts do not fly apart; at most 3000 triangles per scene, shared by its parts.
  • A missing body is a dashed circle of the element size; a missing Effects row is named in the slot.
